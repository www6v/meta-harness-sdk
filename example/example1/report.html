4 · The Surprising Pattern

⚡ Office has the highest AOV — 2.1× the lowest category

Office averages $122.90 per order, while Home averages just $59.49. Despite Office having only 11 orders, its revenue ($1,351.91) is the highest of all categories. A handful of big-ticket items (Ergonomic Chair $389, Standing Desk $299, Bookshelf $189) inflate the average — one Office order is worth roughly 2.1× a Home order.

The bubble chart makes this stark: Office sits in the upper-left (fewer orders, high AOV), while Home clusters toward lower AOV. Office occupies the "sweet spot" of solid volume with strong AOV — the most balanced revenue driver.

Another angle: South's revenue is 59% Office — the most lopsided region-category dependency in the dataset. If Office demand softens, the South region would be disproportionately affected.
